Third Party Logistics

Managing logistics in healthcare can be complex, time-consuming, and resource-intensive. That's why many healthcare providers now rely on Third-Party Logistics partners to handle their distribution and fulfillment needs.

At NDONCO Care, we bring the expertise, infrastructure, and reliability required to take the stress out of your supply chain—so you can focus on what matters most: patient care.

Why Choose NDONCO Care for Your Third Party Logistics Needs?
Customization

No two healthcare businesses are the same, and your logistics solution shouldn't be either. Whether your needs are straightforward or highly complex, NDONCO Care offers tailored logistics solutions to ensure your shipments arrive on time and in perfect condition—every time.

Risk Management

We take full responsibility for quality assurance and risk mitigation. Our team operates with integrity, professionalism, and a strong work ethic. You can trust us to uphold the highest standards in safety, compliance, and service delivery.

Scalability

Healthcare demands change—and we're built to scale with you. Whether you're a small clinic, a growing practice, or a multi-site hospital, NDONCO Care has the resources and flexibility to adapt as your operations expand.

Speed & Reliability

In healthcare, speed saves lives. With over a decade of experience in domestic shipping, we offer:

  • Overnight and next-day delivery to more than 5,000 pin codes via air freight
  • Second- and third-day shipping for less time-sensitive deliveries

Our network ensures rapid and reliable delivery, no matter the urgency.
