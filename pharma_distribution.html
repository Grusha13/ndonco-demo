Pharmaceutical Distribution

NDONCO Care: “Improving Lives Together”

At NDONCO Care, we are committed to supporting your healthcare journey by enhancing the quality of care and service you offer, while also boosting your business profitability. As your trusted pharmaceutical distribution partner, we ensure timely, flexible, and reliable delivery backed by a wide range of branded, generic, and over-the-counter drugs.
With a wide range of customers—including retail chains, independent pharmacies, healthcare institutions, and patients—we provide customized solutions tailored to your needs.
For Hospital Pharmacies

We help streamline hospital pharmacy operations, allowing you to focus on patient care. Our services reduce inventory levels to below 15 days, minimizing holding costs. We also manage expiry and return settlements efficiently, supported by timely reminders.

For Retail Pharmacies

Boost your revenue by expanding patient-centered services. Our pharmacy management systems are built to adapt as your pharmacy grows and diversifies, offering the flexibility you need for modern retail operations.

For Pharmaceutical Manufacturers

We ensure secure, accurate, and timely delivery of pharmaceutical products with specialized logistics solutions designed to meet the rigorous demands of drug manufacturers.

For NDONCO Patients

Never miss a dose with our proactive refill reminder service. Our Customer Care Specialists will contact you via phone or email a week before your medication is due. Orders are only processed once verified by you—no automatic billing or shipping.
