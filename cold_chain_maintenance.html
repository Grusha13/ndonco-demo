Cold Chain Maintenance

Cold Chain Maintenance

At NDONCO Care, we specialize in delivering innovative, reliable cold chain solutions tailored to meet diverse geographic, environmental, and transportation demands. Our unwavering commitment to supply chain integrity and patient safety has positioned us as a trusted leader in refrigerated pharmaceutical logistics.

Our Cold Chain Capabilities
Storage & Cold Rooms

We maintain rigorous temperature control in certified cold rooms (2-8°C) equipped with humidity control systems. These facilities are audited every six months by health authorities and comply with Good Distribution Practices (GDP). Additionally, our operations are independently reviewed quarterly to ensure ongoing compliance and safety.

Packaging & Distribution

We use globally approved cold chain materials to protect the integrity of temperature-sensitive medications during transit. These include:

  • Cool boxes, gel packs, and ice packs
  • Data loggers for real-time temperature monitoring
  • Cold chain and fragile handling labels
  • Durable, validated outer packaging tested by technical university laboratories

Every shipment is customized according to the climatic and geographic conditions of its destination to ensure safe delivery.

Data Recording & Client Information System

Transparency is key to building trust. We provide clients with comprehensive records for each shipment, including:

  • Validation certificates for cold chain packaging.
  • Images of the packing and shipping process
  • Real-time updates and data logger reports

Our system ensures complete visibility from origin to destination.
