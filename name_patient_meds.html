Name Patient Meds

In recent years, the pharmaceutical supply landscape has evolved significantly. One of the most notable changes is the increasing need for imported unlicensed medicines—drugs that are approved in other countries but not yet licensed in India. These medicines can be legally imported under the Name Patient Basis (NPB), specifically for individual patients requiring urgent treatment.

At Super Super Specialities Pharma Warehousing and Logistics Pvt Ltd (SSPWL), we approach the importation of unlicensed medicines with the same core values that define everything we do: quality, value, and trust.

Our operations are backed by:
  • FDA-approved facilities adhering to Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP)
  • A highly skilled, experienced team ensuring regulatory compliance and smooth logistics
  • A commitment to exceptional service, delivering a seamless patient treatment experience

“We provide access to over 2,000 imported medicines through a reliable and trusted global supply network.”

SSPWL stands ready to support patients and healthcare providers with timely, compliant, and compassionate access to life-saving treatments.
